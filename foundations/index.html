01 —Colour

Four colours.

Signal Orange is about 10% of any surface. It marks the action, the live figure, the rising bar — it does not decorate. One orange thing per view. Ink carries all text and every dark surface. Paper is the light ground. Slate is for lines, borders, dividers and disabled states, and never for body text.

02 —Type

Archivo, and a mono that does the work.

Archivo variable, with its width axis. Weight 800 is the wordmark and headline weight; 900 for the largest display; 400–500 for body and labels. The wordmark is set at 88% width — UI text stays at 100%. JetBrains Mono carries every small label and all numeric or technical data, uppercase with 0.16em tracking. That is load-bearing: it is what keeps the identity feeling like infrastructure rather than a flyer.

03 —Spacing & grid

Square, on a 4px rhythm.

Left-aligned, generous whitespace, hairline rules rather than boxes. Two-column and four-column grids with 16–20px gaps. Corners are square everywhere; the only radius in the identity is the app icon tile at 22% of its side.

04 —Motion

Things enter by rising.

Entrances travel 8px upward and stagger in sequence like the bars of the mark, 45ms between siblings. 140ms for state changes, 200ms for entrances, 320ms only for a full view transition. cubic-bezier(0.16, 0.84, 0.44, 1) — a firm ease-out, no bounce, no spring. Nothing scales on hover; nothing rotates.

05 —Iconography

Lucide, squared.

Lucide ships round caps and joins by default. CSS wins over the presentation attributes in the SVGs, so squaring them costs one rule and brings the icon set in line with an identity that has no round corners anywhere except the app icon tile. Sizes 16, 20 and 24, on the same 4px rhythm as everything else.

.icon { stroke-width: 1.5; stroke-linecap: square; stroke-linejoin: miter; }
Squared — correct

Mitred joins, square terminals. Sits with the sheared bars of the mark.

Lucide default — override it

Rounded caps and joins. Reads as a different, softer system.

Icons are ink, or slate when secondary. One icon per view may be orange — the same discipline as everything else. Icons are never filled, never boxed in a circle or a tinted tile, and never used decoratively beside a heading. Emoji are not used. Unicode symbols are not used as icons. Where a glyph is genuinely unavailable in Lucide, add it as a hand-built SVG on the same grid and stroke, and document it — do not swap in a second icon library.

06 —The logo

Four bars rising off a stage line.

Bar width 16 units, gap 8, shear 10. The first three bars step 16 units each; the orange bar steps 18, so it clears the wordmark's cap line by a hair and is the only element above the name. The wordmark sits on the stage line with its cap height locked to the third bar's top point, 15 units to the right of the mark. It reads as a rising signal, a waveform, a stage, and a growth curve at once.

Primary — ink + orange, for paper and light photography
Knockout — paper + orange, for ink and dark photography
Mono ink — on orange the whole mark goes ink. Also for stamps, faxes, engraving.

07 —Imagery

Real rooms, underexposed.

Live event photography, in colour, underexposed. Warm stage light, deep shadow, and real rooms — shot from inside the crowd or the booth rather than from a balcony. No duotone, no orange wash, no filters, no grain overlay: the frames already carry warmth that sits with the orange, and tinting them would spend the signal colour on decoration.

Ink scrim at 70%, or set the type in the darkest third of the frame
Full-bleed to the grid edge, never inset in a rounded card. Captions are mono, slate, 11px caps.

08 —Voice

Claims come with a figure attached.

Do
  • Section headlines in caps, declarative, often two beats.
  • Body in sentence case, short, factual, numbers-forward.
  • Verbs of motion: push, drive, amplify, reach, convert.
  • The company is “we”; the organiser is “you”.
  • Name things plainly: The Promo Dashboard, The Heat Map, Influencer Bridge, Campaign Credits.
Don't
  • No emoji.
  • No exclamation marks.
  • No “revolutionary” or “game-changing”.
  • No claim without a figure attached.
