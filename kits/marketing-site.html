The organiser-facing marketing page, recreated from RiserEvents' HomeHeroSection and HomeMarketingSections. Every headline and every block of copy below is the site's own — “The Push Engine.”, “What the giants forgot.”, the six organiser types, the 8% flat fee. What changed is the setting: mono section labels as the organising device, hairline rules instead of cards with radius and shadow, and one orange per section rather than a blue-to-purple gradient.

01 —Hero
components/HomeHeroSection.tsx

The hero's marquee of event tiles is kept — it is the one piece of motion on the page that earns its place, since a wall of live events is the argument. It scrolls at a constant rate rather than easing, and it pauses on hover and under prefers-reduced-motion. The word PUSHED was set in the old primary blue; it is the orange now, and it is the section's only orange — which is why Sign up here is a solid paper fill rather than the primary button it is in the closing block.
