Design system — components
Foundations Components Promo kit Buyer kit Marketing kit

Every primitive, with its states. Each one is recreated from the two codebases rather than invented: RiserEvents is canonical for shared primitives, RiserPromo supplies the dashboard components Events does not have. Where a component existed in both, it is here once, with the Promo difference folded in as a prop. Prop contracts are in components/*.tsx; the migration notes are in docs/MIGRATION.md.

01 —Button

Sizes are Events' contract exactly: sm, md, lg. primary is the orange — one per view. Hover darkens the fill or fills the rule over 140ms; nothing scales.

Variants — primary · ink · secondary · ghost · danger · disabled
Sizes — 28 · 36 · 44px, on the 4px rhythm
On ink — secondary and ghost rules invert
02 —StatusBadge

Mono caps over a 2px rule. Both codebases had drifted — Events used rounded-full pills, Promo had a commented-out six-shade grey ramp. Six greys cannot be told apart at 11px, and the brand has one signal colour, so only live spends the orange.

05 —Card

Paper

1px hairline on paper. Square corners, no shadow. The default surface.

Ink

A solid ink block. Rules invert automatically inside it.

Dashed

Measurement and specification only. Never decoration.

Accent

Rare. Ink on orange is 7.3:1. Everything inside goes ink.

06 —DataGrid

Events' implementation is canonical, including its responsive breakpoint algorithm — that is not styling, it decides which columns exist at a given width. Promo's copy was identical except that it fetched team membership inside the component and rendered a banner; that is now the notice prop.
